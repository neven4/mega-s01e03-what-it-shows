Separate the fixes

Shared parser

For a day-first tenant, app/email reports and forms without the conflicting hint are assigned to parser. The shared parser reads month first. For example, R-41205 from morrow.tax reports 3 October becoming 10 March. That tenant uses fr-FR, although the reporter’s browser is en-US and intake guessed hint.

Fix the parser to follow the tenant’s date order, then test ambiguous inputs such as 03/10/2026 and unambiguous days above twelve. Keep these reports in the parser ticket. The timing exceptions listed below still need investigation; do not promise that this fix resolves them.

Booking-v3 date hint

For a day-first tenant using booking-v3, a report through form is assigned to hint. That form displays MM/DD/YYYY but reads the tenant’s day-first format. R-41230 from copper.studio reports 6 January becoming 1 June, despite intake tagging it parser.

Make the displayed hint agree with the form’s tenant-specific interpretation. Test the form and hint together, including day-first and month-first locales. This needs its own ticket and verification; changing the shared parser does not correct the hint.

  • Different subjects can describe the same cause: app and email from kettle.books.
  • The same subject can hide different causes: form and app from alder.physio.
  • The same subject and channel can hide different causes: copper.studio and morrow.tax forms.
  • Unknown tenants are unplaced; a matching subject does not justify merging them.

Deduplicate export rows by report ID first. Then read each body and look up the tenant’s locale, booking-form version, and entry channel. Use the classifier as evidence, check for contradicting symptoms, and merge only when the underlying problem matches. Keep uncertain reports for investigation rather than forcing them into a familiar cause.

Engineering should track each confirmed cause separately, retain each report ID and its assignment, and verify each fix against representative inputs. Re-read and render this page when new exports arrive: these components recompute their results from the files at build time.
